# Evaluate an existing arrangement

Read the rules and inspect actual use. Identify the resource, participants, needs, benefits, costs, decision rights, and existing commitments. Diagnose scarcity, upkeep, conflicting incentives, uncertainty, or coordination before recommending more rules.

Compare the arrangement with its purpose:
- Are access, responsibilities, and release obligations clear and locally workable?
- Are contention, exceptions, care, and handoffs handled fairly?
- Can affected participants challenge decisions, amend rules, and leave? What costs or obligations limit those options?
- Are monitoring and responses proportionate, and disputes inexpensive to resolve?
- Do capacity, waiting, waste, pressure, useful results, and coordination burden support the claimed outcome?

Distinguish proposed, agreed, implemented, and observed-to-work arrangements. Missing evidence is uncertainty, not proof of success or failure. Monitor relevant resource use without unnecessary surveillance of participants.

Return consequential gaps, supporting observations, uncertainty, and the smallest warranted changes. If changes are wanted, continue through Design; if alternatives need investigation, use Explore.
