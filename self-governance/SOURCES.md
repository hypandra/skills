# Self-governance: sources and provenance

- [Elinor Ostrom’s Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/lecture/): self-governed commons and polycentric governance; a design lens, not evidence for a particular software architecture.
- [Windags commons-governance skill](https://github.com/curiositech/windags-skills/blob/main/skills/ostrom-commons-governance/SKILL.md): audits against Ostrom’s principles, including collective choice, proportionate responses, and nested governance.
- [AIOS commons-governance skill](https://github.com/The-AIOS/aios/blob/main/skills/aios/commons-governance/SKILL.md): compact, locally fitted rules for shared spaces.
- [Cursor pstack: Separate Before Serializing Shared State](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/principle-separate-before-serializing-shared-state/SKILL.md): reduce unnecessary sharing and distinguish conventions from structural controls.
- [Zintellix swarm skill, inspected commit](https://github.com/Zintellix/Claude-Skills/blob/695e9dbe52b8f322426df195af2cb276e6d2eed6/skills/swarm/SKILL.md): complementary responsibilities, evidence, and synthesis; its fixed team size and architecture are not adopted.

An earlier source revision received an independent review with no blocking findings. The later changes below have not received a new independent review. No operational enforcement or measured benefits are established.

## Rights, authority, and changes to arrangements

Daniel’s November 4 wording refinement distinguishes meaningful participant voice and decision rights, effects on those affected, and respect for existing rights, obligations, and legitimate authority—including when seeking to change them. Participants may propose or petition for change; implementation still needs applicable permission. This draft update has not received a new independent review; the latest independent PASS covers its predecessor only. No installation, activation, or measured operational benefit is established.
